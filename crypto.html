Crypto Market Overview

Bitcoin, Ethereum and major cryptocurrencies continue to react to liquidity cycles, institutional activity, network usage and macro sentiment.

Bitcoin

BTC/USD

Large macro trend dynamics, liquidity flows and post-halving sentiment shape market behaviour.

Ethereum

ETH/USD

Network activity, staking demand and market structure influence broader crypto sentiment.

Major alts

Solana, Cardano, XRP and others

Risk appetite and sector rotation can create sharp relative moves across digital assets.

Metrics

Crypto themes

Dominance

Market share shifts between Bitcoin and altcoins can influence rotation.

Volatility

Crypto often moves faster than traditional markets and requires disciplined risk management.

Trend

News

Risk management
