About PLATINUM RETURN OF MARKET

“PLATINUM RETURN OF MARKET is a market research and trading-focused platform dedicated to providing structured insights across Forex, Crypto, Gold, Indices and global financial markets.

Led by Amit Chaudhary, Chief Executive Officer, the platform focuses on market analysis, trading education, technical research and disciplined risk-management practices.

Our objective is to help market participants understand financial markets through structured information, analytical frameworks and educational resources.

We believe that successful participation in financial markets requires knowledge, discipline, proper risk management and a long-term approach—not promises of guaranteed returns.”
